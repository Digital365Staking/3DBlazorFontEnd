<b>/* Proposal for Enhancing the Application Architecture of SharePoint Web Applications */</b>

<h4>Why Avoid SharePoint Web Parts for Business Applications?</h4>

<p>
        SharePoint Web Parts are an excellent way to extend SharePoint,
        but they quickly become limiting when they are expected to support
        significant business logic.
    </p>

    <ul>
        <li>Dependency on the SharePoint lifecycle.</li>
        <li>Limited user experience.</li>
        <li>Tightly coupled architecture.</li>
        <li>More complex deployment processes.</li>
        <li>Limited reusability outside the SharePoint environment.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>
        As the application grows, these limitations become increasingly apparent.
    </p>



<p>
Microsoft 365 and SharePoint provide a robust foundation for document management,
collaboration, and secure access through Microsoft Entra ID, Single Sign-On (SSO),
and centralized permission management.
</p>

<p>
In addition, <strong>Blazor</strong> enables the development of modern business applications,
offering greater design flexibility, a richer user experience,
and a far more scalable architecture than solutions built directly
as SharePoint Web Parts.
</p>





<p>
The proposed approach is therefore not to replace SharePoint,
but to fully leverage its strengths as a document management
and collaboration platform while delegating business functionality
to Blazor applications that integrate seamlessly with the Microsoft 365 ecosystem.
</p>

<p>
We would be delighted to discuss this vision with you and explore
the migration strategies best suited to your organization's needs.
</p>
